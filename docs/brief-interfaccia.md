## Scope
Main mail screen of Better-Look, an open-source desktop email client for Italian SMEs. Visitor mode: Operate. Artboards: onboarding prototype, classic and new views, File backstage, compose in window and in reading pane, settings (appearance, accounts and sync), after-send state, dark theme, phone list.

## Audience and task
Italian office staff who use Outlook daily; triage, read, reply, forward, archive, flag. Positions, shortcuts and the overall color feel must match what their hands and eyes expect. Name, logo and icons stay original. All mailbox content is synthetic.

## Direction contract
THESIS (user-directed, replaces the postal-desk world): familiar Outlook feel in both classic and new views, so users switch without retraining. No stamps, postmarks or airmail decoration.
OWN-WORLD: Fluent 2 neutral and brand ramps (MIT). Light: ground #F0F0F0, white cards, brand #0F6CBD, blue header bar with white search. Dark: neutral greys #141414 / #292929, brand ink #479EF5. Unread = 3px brand bar plus brand subject. Ribbon icons tinted by function (brand, green archive, red flag/junk, amber quick actions). System font stack led by Segoe UI. Radius 4px controls, 8px cards. Avatar tints by name hash.
IDENTITY: Name "Better-Look": a better alternative for people coming from Outlook. Provisional logo: "Better-Look" wordmark in a geometric sans (letters in currentColor, hyphen in the accent color) and a blue rounded-square app icon with a white "B". User decision: the logo never appears in the working interface. It lives only on the "Informazioni su Better-Look" page (File backstage in classic, Settings in new). The header shows no name at all: the app should feel built into the OS, branding is not a goal.
STORY: An Outlook user finds New top-left, the command bar across the top (extended or compact, user choice), search centered, folders left, list center, reading pane right, reply commands top-right of the letter.
SEND: immediate, with a "Messaggio inviato" toast and Annulla.

## Unresolved
